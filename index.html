About Stippling

The Patience of Ten Hundred Thousand Dots

Precision Micro-Pointillism

Stippling is an arduous, devotional drawing technique where form, tone, and atmospheric shadow are created entirely through individual ink dots. By modulating dot size, proximity, and pressure, smooth velvet gradients emerge from stark white tooth to impenetrable obsidian darkness.

August L. Soloman combines traditional 0.05mm technical pens on archival vellum with custom-configured pressure-sensitive stipple engines in Clip Studio Paint. Each masterwork requires between 80 to 200 hours of continuous dot placement.

The result is a tactile, fine-grain texture that gives eerie macabre entities and anatomical structures an unsettling, atmospheric presence.
